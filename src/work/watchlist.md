A full-stack web app for searching (powered by TMDB), saving, and filtering films you've seen or want to watch. It grew out of my partner & I just needing a place for this for ourselves! It has a public list view with editing gated behind an auth. The frontend is built with Vue3 and TypeScript, and backend is built with Python (FastAPI) and PostgreSQL.
